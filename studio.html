    How it works & what to expect
    Paste a link, or bring your own
    YouTube links: the Mac helper downloads audio and converts it into individual MP3s in Downloads/NEEDLEDROP. Save MP3: download the original file without re-encoding. Make MP3: extract the first audio track from a file. Record tab: capture playback as you listen, including any other sounds from that tab.
    Some sources stay out of reach
    Protected-media detection does not block recording. Chrome or the service may still restrict capture or provide silence; restricted browser pages cannot be captured. Adaptive streams and blob URLs use real-time recording rather than direct file conversion. This extension does not remove DRM or bypass access controls.
    Your own little recording desk
    No NEEDLEDROP account, analytics or remote conversion. The Mac helper connects to YouTube and its media servers. Local files and tab recordings are processed inside Chrome. A conversion may ask for permission to its host. Use recordings you own or have permission to save.
