How it works & what to expect

Three ways to get a track

Save MP3: download the original file without re-encoding. Make MP3: extract the first audio track from a file. Record tab: capture playback as you listen, including any other sounds from that tab.

Some sources stay out of reach

Protected-media detection does not block recording. Chrome or the service may still restrict capture or provide silence; restricted browser pages cannot be captured. Adaptive streams and blob URLs use real-time recording rather than direct file conversion. This extension does not remove DRM or bypass access controls.

Your own little recording desk

No account, analytics, uploads or remote conversion. Network access is used to fetch the media you choose. A conversion may ask for permission to its host. Use recordings you own or have permission to save.
